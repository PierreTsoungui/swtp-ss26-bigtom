## DMP Analysis

**Differentially Methylated Positions (DMPs)** represent individual CpG sites
showing statistically significant methylation differences between biological
groups.

The analysis reports:

- p-values
- adjusted p-values
- methylation differences (Delta Beta)
- effect sizes

### Interpreting the results

Because hundreds of thousands of CpGs are tested simultaneously, **adjusted
p-values** should generally be used when interpreting significance.

A positive Delta Beta indicates higher methylation in the first group, whereas a
negative Delta Beta indicates higher methylation in the second group.
